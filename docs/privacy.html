Hype Atlas Bot: Privacy Policy & Data Deletion

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Hype Atlas Bot is a private automation tool used only by the owner of the Facebook Page Hype Atlas - Viral & Trending (facebook.com/hypeatlasnews), its Instagram account and its YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can log in to it.

What the app does

  • Finds publicly trending news topics (public news feeds and public Wikipedia pages).
  • Writes a short post and creates an image or a short video for it.
  • Publishes that post on the Hype Atlas Facebook Page and Instagram account, and uploads the same short video to the owner's own YouTube channel as a YouTube Short.
  • Reads the Page's own post statistics (views, reactions, comments, shares) to learn which topics the audience likes, so future posts are more relevant.
  • May read and reply to comments left on the Page's own posts.

What data the app uses

  • The Page access token that the Page owner granted to the app.
  • For YouTube: an access token for the owner's own YouTube channel with the youtube.upload permission, used only to upload the channel's own videos with their title, description and tags.
  • Public post statistics and comments on the Hype Atlas Page.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not collect, store or sell personal information about anyone.
  • It does not access any user's profile, friends, messages, YouTube account or private data.
  • It does not share any data with third parties or use it for advertising.
  • Comment text is processed only to understand audience interest and to write replies; it is not stored in any user-identifiable form.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The YouTube access is used only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel. YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Data storage

The app keeps only a list of topics it has already posted (to avoid repeats) and aggregate performance numbers per post. No personal data about users is stored.

Data deletion

Because the app does not store personal data about users, there is nothing to delete. If you have interacted with the Hype Atlas Page and want anything removed (for example a reply the Page made to your comment), send a message to the Page at facebook.com/hypeatlasnews and it will be deleted within 30 days.
